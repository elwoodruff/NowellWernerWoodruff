Cal Fire 25 Metadata

This dataset contains the Cal Fire dataset (https://www.fire.ca.gov/what-we-do/fire-resource-assessment-program/fire-perimeters) from years 1878 - 2025 in the state of California, looking at both presribed burns and accidental fires.

We are going to focus on the data of cause, acres affected, year, length of burn (calculated from alarm date to containment date), and treatment. 

There are no permissions or restrictions on this data; it is available to the public.
